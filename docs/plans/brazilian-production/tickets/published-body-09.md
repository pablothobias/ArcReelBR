## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Use the prepared reference dossier and formal episode; reuse assets, generation, editing and export operations.
- [ ] Apply Codex-only spending and quality-attempt controls throughout and preserve recoverable state.
- [ ] Observe actual native audio before deciding whether a separate voice path is required; if ElevenLabs or alignment work is necessary, report the exact failure and open a separately scoped follow-up rather than falsely passing the pilot.
- [ ] Confirm reference intent, Brazilian naturalness, dialogue completeness, continuity, subtitles and final playback, with evidence and material limitations.
- [ ] Deliver vertical 1080 × 1920 with burned Portuguese subtitles, title, description, source information and recorded spending; do not publish to social platforms.
- [ ] Complete only when the final output passes review; a blocked voice/alignment issue is not success and no unbounded generation is allowed.
