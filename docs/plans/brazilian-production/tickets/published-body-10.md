## What to build

After the single-video pilot passes, Codex produces a bounded two-episode continuity sample using existing project state and makes the remaining request collection ready for controlled production rounds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reuse shared characters, scene/prop assets, voice settings, overview, episode ledger and current/next outlines; no parallel series bible.
- [ ] Choose a source-supported two-episode case and verify narrative and identity continuity against existing source and formal-script facts.
- [ ] Obtain a quote and numeric allowance for this new round; the pilot allowance does not authorize all 30 requests.
- [ ] Prepare a status inventory of the remaining requests with verified/blocked references, adaptation readiness and supported production path.
- [ ] Demonstrate stopping and resuming between items without duplicate submissions and without exceeding Codex-approved bounds.
- [ ] Document actual limitations and file narrowly scoped issues only for evidenced gaps; do not automatically generate the entire collection.
