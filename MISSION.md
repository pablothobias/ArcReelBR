# Mission: Understand ArcReelBR

## Why
Understand what ArcReelBR can produce today, identify useful next improvements, and learn enough about the project to develop it.

## Success looks like
- Choose an appropriate production path and prepare its inputs.
- Distinguish implemented behavior from limitations and unverified behavior.
- Identify the main parts of the code when planning development.

## Constraints
- Explain simply and directly, in Brazilian Portuguese, without assumptions.
- Base claims on the current repository and identify the reviewed commit.

## Out of scope
- Unrequested changes to product behavior or paid media generation.
